## 🧐 Critical Thinking: 10 Data Analysis Questions
*Answer these using the provided CSV and Dashboard to test your understanding of the data.*

1. **Portfolio Distribution:** Which region accounts for the highest total portfolio value?
2. **Risk Assessment:** What is the correlation between 'Income Level' and 'Delinquency Status'?
3. **Credit Health:** What percentage of customers fall under the 'Very Poor' credit rating?
4. **Employment Impact:** Are 'Unemployed' individuals more likely to be in the 'High Risk Segment' compared to 'Salaried' individuals?
5. **Gender Analysis:** Is there a significant difference in the 'Total Amount of Risk' between Male and Female customers?
6. **Delinquency Deep-Dive:** What is the average credit score of customers marked as 'Delinquent'?
7. **Segmentation:** Which combination of 'Employment Type' and 'Income Level' represents the safest customer profile?
8. **Geographic Risk:** Does the 'South' region's high exposure stem from a large number of customers or a few high-value high-risk accounts?
9. **Score Thresholds:** If the minimum credit score for 'Good' was raised to 750, how many customers would be downgraded?
10. **Business Strategy:** Based on the 58% Delinquency Rate, what specific policy change would you recommend for the 'High Risk Segment'?
